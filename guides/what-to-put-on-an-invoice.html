What to put on an invoice (freelancer's checklist)

Invoices bounce back for boring reasons: a missing PO number, no due date, unclear payment instructions. Every bounce restarts the payment clock. The fix is a complete invoice the first time.

The essentials

  1. The word "Invoice" and a unique invoice number. Sequential numbering (1041, 1042…) keeps you sane and looks established; date-based schemes (2026-014) work too. Never reuse a number.
  2. Your details: name/business name, address, email, and any tax or registration ID your jurisdiction requires.
  3. Their details: the legal entity name and, for larger clients, the accounts-payable contact — not just your day-to-day contact, who may never see it.
  4. Issue date and due date. "Due on receipt" sounds fast but is vague; "Due [date] (net-15)" is a date someone can be late against. Not sure which term to quote? See payment terms explained: net 30 vs net 15 vs due on receipt.
  5. Line items: description, quantity/hours, rate, line total. Mirror the language of the proposal so approval is instant recognition, not detective work.
  6. Totals: subtotal, any tax you're required to charge, the final amount, and the currency (spell it out — USD, EUR — for cross-border clients).
  7. How to pay: exact instructions for at least one method — account details for a transfer or a payment link. Every extra step a payer must ask about costs days.

Small details that speed up payment

  • PO number, if the client issued one. Large companies' systems reject invoices without it.
  • Late terms, stated plainly, if you use them ("2% monthly on balances 30+ days past due") — they matter less as fees than as a signal you notice lateness. Our late-fee guide covers how much to charge and the exact wording.
  • A one-line thank-you. Free, and accounts-payable humans are humans.

Then track what you sent

An invoice you can't find three weeks later is an invoice you won't chase. Log every invoice the moment it goes out — number, client, amount, due date — somewhere with an automatic overdue flag.
