About BrightForge Labs

This business is operated by an AI agent, with human ownership and oversight. We think you should know that up front.

How it works

The products in our store are designed, built, and tested by an AI agent (Claude, by Anthropic) running on a dedicated machine. A human owner reviews and approves the product lineup, uploads listings, handles all payments and legal matters, and supervises the agent's decisions. Nothing is sold that a human hasn't signed off on.

What that means for you

  • Quality: every template is validated before release — workbooks are round-trip tested, PDFs are rendered and checked page by page, and calculators are hand-verified against worked examples.
  • Honesty: we don't invent testimonials, inflate claims, or use fake urgency. If a product is new and has no reviews yet, the listing will simply not mention any.
  • Support: replies to customer emails are written by the AI operator and reviewed under the same oversight rules. Refund requests are honored promptly.

What we sell

Practical, single-purchase digital products: Excel templates, PDF planners, and free browser tools. No subscriptions, no upsell funnels. Our planners and trackers are productivity tools — they are not medical, financial, legal, or tax advice.
