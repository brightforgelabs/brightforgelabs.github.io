How to chase a late invoice (with email templates)

Most late invoices aren't disputes — they're forgotten. Which means the first reminder can be friendly, short, and sent the day after the due date, not three awkward weeks later.

The escalation ladder

Three emails cover almost every case. Each one stays polite and adds one notch of firmness. Send them on a schedule, not on a mood.

Day 1 past due — the nudge

Subject: Invoice #1042 — friendly reminder

Hi [Name],

Just a quick note that invoice #1042 for [project], $[amount], was due yesterday ([due date]). It may well already be in motion — if so, please ignore this. If it slipped through, here's the invoice again [attach/link].

Thanks! [You]

One week past due — the check-in

Subject: Invoice #1042 now a week past due

Hi [Name],

Following up on invoice #1042 ($[amount], due [due date]) — I haven't seen the payment come through yet. Could you let me know its status, and whether anything on my end is holding it up (a missing PO number, a different billing contact)?

Appreciate it, [You]

Three weeks past due — the firm one

Subject: Overdue invoice #1042 — payment plan needed

Hi [Name],

Invoice #1042 ($[amount]) is now three weeks past due and I've checked in twice. I'd like to get this resolved this week — can you confirm a payment date by Friday? If there's a problem with the invoice itself, tell me and I'll fix it same-day. Per my terms, work on [next deliverable] is paused until the account is current.

Regards, [You]

Pausing work is the leverage most freelancers actually have. State it as policy, not as a threat — and only if it is your stated policy on the invoice or contract.

The part that prevents the next one

  • Know the day an invoice goes late. Reminders only work if you notice. A tracker with an automatic overdue flag turns "oh no, was that paid?" into a red row you see Monday morning.
  • Shorter terms: net-15 gets forgotten less than net-30 — here's how to pick the right payment term per client.
  • A stated late-fee policy: even a modest one makes the due date mean something — see how much to charge and how to word it.
  • Invoice immediately: the closer the invoice lands to the delivered work, the faster it's paid.
  • A clearer invoice email up front: an amount and a real due date in the subject and body means fewer invoices lost in inboxes — the invoice email guide covers the format and templates.
