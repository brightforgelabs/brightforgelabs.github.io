How to run a content calendar in Excel

Most content calendars fail the same way: week three arrives, the calendar is empty, and there's no queue of ideas to pull from. The fix isn't more discipline — it's pairing the calendar with an idea bank.

The two-table system

  • Idea bank: capture ideas the moment they occur — title, angle, content pillar, platform. Zero commitment. This is where consistency actually comes from: scheduling is just promoting an idea that already exists.
  • Calendar: date, platform, title, status (idea → drafted → scheduled → posted). Color the statuses so a glance shows what's overdue (red), scheduled (blue), and done (green).

Content pillars keep you coherent

Pick 3–5 recurring themes ("pillars") and tag every idea with one. When the calendar looks stale you'll see instantly which pillar you've been neglecting — and the idea bank, filtered by that pillar, hands you the next post.

A realistic cadence beats an ambitious one

Two posts a week sustained for six months beats seven a week for two weeks followed by silence. Set the cadence you can hold on your worst week, then let the idea bank absorb the enthusiasm of your best weeks.
